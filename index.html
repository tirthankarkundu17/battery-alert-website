Why the 20-80% Rule Matters

Lithium-ion batteries experience the most stress when fully charged (above 80%) or deeply discharged (below 20%). Staying in this sweet spot can double your battery's service life.

Avoid Thermal Stress

Charging past 80% creates excess heat, which degrades internal battery chemistry rapidly. Our alarm reminds you to unplug.

Reduce Cycle Counts

A full 0% to 100% charge uses 1 complete cycle, whereas partial charges use only a fraction, heavily extending longevity.

Long-term Longevity

Regular threshold usage maintains your battery capacity at near-factory levels even after years of daily operation.

Battery Maintenance Tips

Extend Longevity with Simple Habits

Avoid costly battery replacements with these professional care practices directly integrated within Battery Alert.

01

Stay out of the red zone

Letting your battery hit 0% stresses the anode and cathode materials. Recharge once it falls to 20%.

02

Unplug at 80% charge

Leaving the charger plugged in at 100% subjects cells to high-voltage stress, boosting physical corrosion.

03

Avoid temperature spikes

Heat is a battery's worst enemy. Never charge under direct sunlight, or run heavy gaming apps during fast charges.

04

Use slower charging overnight

If charging overnight, use a standard slow charger. Fast chargers pump current rapidly, heating the battery.
